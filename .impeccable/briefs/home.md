# Surface brief: frontend/src/pages (Home + boards)

Scope: whole app; mode Persuade on Home hero, Operate on the boards and claim flow, Read on policy pages.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The leaderboard IS the ad. The first viewport shows the live auction for #1, priced in rupees, with the top rows already glowing beneath it. It refuses the SaaS hero-plus-feature-cards scaffold.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black (#07070a) ground (dark only), hairline borders, one sky-blue accent (#38bdf8) applied as light; cursor-following 3D neon tubes behind the first viewport; soft offset glows, beam line under the active nav, accent-tinted top-3 rows. Display face Bricolage Grotesque, body Schibsted Grotesk, amounts in Spline Sans Mono tabular. Icons from lucide, one stroke weight.
STORY: Visitor sees who is #1 and the exact rupee price to take it, understands the rule in one line, and claims a rank or explores a category.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Slim header with live strip (online, visitors today). Category chip rail. Board tabs. Centered "Claim #1 for − ₹X +" stepper headline at display scale, claim form row beneath (URL, category, Claim rank). Ranked rows start immediately below with #1–#3 accent-tinted; sidebar with Deals and Today mini-boards from lg.
FORM: pinned by the user. seed: none (pinned) — "dark background with carefully balanced neon accents", "interactive color palette … apply the selected color consistently", "Aceternity/Skiper-style near-black UI, light-based motion".
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the price stepper. Each ± pulses a light sweep across the amount and the claim button; the #1 row's glow intensity follows the amount you are offering relative to the current leader.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
